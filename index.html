Common Questions

You Ask, We Answer

Are your products cruelty-free?
Absolutely. We never test on animals, and we only work with suppliers who adhere to the same strict ethical standards. We are Leaping Bunny certified.
Are these products suitable for sensitive skin?
Yes! Our formulas are designed with sensitive skin in mind, avoiding harsh chemicals, synthetic fragrances, and known irritants. We recommend patch testing any new product.
What is your shipping policy?
We offer free shipping on all orders over $50. Standard delivery typically takes 3-5 business days. International shipping options are available at checkout.
Can I use the oil if I have oily skin?
Contrary to popular belief, face oils can actually help balance oily skin by regulating sebum production. Our Jojoba-based oil mimics your skin's natural oils.
